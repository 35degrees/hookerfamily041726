# Samuel Talcott Hooker — lines to the Founders of Hartford

Research record compiled 8 October 2026. **27 direct lines to 22 Founders of Hartford.**

Founder status follows the Society of the Descendants of the Founders of Hartford (SDFH) list. Generations below the canonical tree come from FamilySearch, WikiTree, FindAGrave and the SDFH founder pages, as pasted during research. Most of these upper generations are **not built in the tree**: in-law lines stay terminal. This file is the only place the full chains are recorded.

Status key:
- **tree**: every link is wired in `canonical.json`.
- **CC**: the founder is in the tree and linked by a vertical CC, but the middle generations are not built.
- **research**: traced in outside sources, not in the tree.

## The two trunks

Every line starts down one of these. Years are birth years.

**Paternal trunk (P):**
Samuel Talcott Hooker (1979) → Michael Gay Hooker (1935) → Rodman Lent Hooker (1909) → Robert Gay Hooker (1862) → Charles Gay Hooker (1822) → Henry Clay Hooker Sr. (1791) → Seth Hooker (1759)

Seth Hooker's parents are Rev. John Hooker (1729) and Sarah Worthington (1732). Rev. John's parents are John Hooker (1695) and **Mercy Hart (1703)**. John Hooker (1695)'s parents are Hon. John Hooker (1664) and **Abigail Standley (1669)**.

**Maternal trunk (M):**
Samuel Talcott Hooker (1979) → Lea Austen Hooker (1946) → Helen Hope Peet (1914) → Florence Hazen Talcott Hope (1881) → John Butler Talcott (1824)

John Butler Talcott's parents are Seth Talcott (1801) and **Charlotte Stout Butler (1804)**. Seth Talcott's parents are Samuel Talcott III (1765) and **Abigail Pantry Hooker (1770)**. Abigail's parents are Thomas Hart Hooker (1745) and Sarah Whitman. Thomas Hart Hooker's parents are Roger Hooker (1710) and **Mercy Hart (1719)**. Roger's parents are Hon. John Hooker (1664) and Abigail Standley (1669), so the two trunks meet there.

## Cousin: Ulysses S. Grant

The shared ancestors are **Samuel Grant Sr. (1631–1718) and Mary Porter (1637–1681)** of Windsor. Samuel was the son of Matthew Grant (1601–1681), a Windsor settler. Matthew is not the Hartford founder Seth Grant.

- **Grant's line:** Ulysses S. Grant (1822) → Jesse Root Grant (1794) → Noah Grant III (1748–1819) → Noah Grant II (1718–1756) → Noah Grant (1693–1727) → Samuel Grant (1659–1710) → Samuel Grant Sr. and Mary Porter.
- **Sam's line:** **M** → Florence Hazen Talcott Hope (1881) → Fannie Hall Hazen Talcott (1849) → Fanny Lucinda Grant Hazen (1821–1893) → Dr. John Grant (1791–1827) → Miner Grant (1756–1828) → Capt. Oliver Grant Jr. (1729–1798) → Oliver Grant (1703–1757) → Josiah Grant (1668–1732) → Samuel Grant Sr. and Mary Porter.

Sam is 11 generations below the couple and Grant is 6, so they are **fifth cousins five times removed**. Fanny Lucinda Grant (b. 1821) was Ulysses Grant's (b. 1822) fifth cousin exactly.

## Dead ends and ruled out

- **Hannah Edwards Butler (1676–1742):** descends from John Edwards of Wethersfield, not founder William Edwards.
- **Rebecca Standish line:** the Standishes of Wethersfield are not on the founder list.
- **Mary Belden Wright (1687–1769):** the Belden and Chittenden families are not on the list.
- **Hannah Morgan Goodwin (1703):** Morgan and Merry of Northampton.
- **Hannah Moseley Talcott (1709):**
  - The Moseleys and Newberrys were Windsor and Westfield families.
  - Her mother's Root line descends from John Root of Farmington, who was the founder Thomas Root's brother, not Thomas himself.
- **Lt. John Worthington (1679):** his mother was Susannah Williamson, not a Bunce. His father's line goes back to England.
- **Fannie Hall Hazen's Hazen side:** Rowley, Massachusetts.
- **Fannie Hall Hazen's Grant side:**
  - Mary Porter's father is John Porter of Windsor, not founder Thomas Porter.
  - FamilySearch's Richard Billings (1590–1640) is not founder Richard Billings, who died at Hatfield in 1679. That Billings link is unproven.
- **Mehitabel Porter Goodwin:** a John Porter of Windsor descendant, so no Thomas Porter line and no third Hart line through her.
- **Hannah Hart Cowles (Sarah Bull's grandmother):** her WikiTree profile says she was not Stephen Hart's daughter.
- **Mary Smith Talcott (1733):** FamilySearch stops at Samuel Smith (b. 1708). Her mother was Sarah Goodwin, per the tree note, and is untested.
- **Irene Allis Ensign's Scott line:** FamilySearch misattaches William Scott (1635–1718) of Hatfield to John Scott and Sarah Bliss, who married in 1659. His real parents are unknown. If found, they could lead to Thomas Scott or Thomas Bliss.

## Open leads

- **Mary Smith Talcott's mother, Sarah Goodwin:** a possible second Goodwin line.
- **William Scott of Hatfield's real parents:** a possible Scott or Bliss line.
- **The Pantry generations** between Abigail (Pantry) Whitman and William Pantry.
- **A second line to the founder John Talcott:** none found. Hannah Wright's Wrights are not the Glastonbury Wright–Talcott branch, and the Hazen–Grant side runs through Rowley, Windsor and Stonington.
